# ChronoMap 3D - thanks for helping us test

This is the sheet you hand to a participant. Print it, or send it as a link.

---

## Before we start

We are testing a **design**, not you. If something is confusing or you get stuck, that is exactly
what we need to find out - it tells us the design has a problem, not that you did anything wrong.
There are no right answers and nothing you do here is scored.

It takes about **25 minutes in person**, or about **15 minutes on your own**.

You can stop at any point, for any reason, without explaining why.

---

## What ChronoMap 3D is

ChronoMap 3D is an app where people who are at the same place photograph it together during a short
time window, and the photos are combined into one 3D model of that place.

Imagine you are standing at the University of Melbourne clock tower on a sunny afternoon. Three
other people you have never met are there with the app, doing the same thing.

**One important thing:** this is a prototype, so the other people are staged. Things will sometimes
happen on their own - the group filling up, or the host sending you an instruction. Please treat
those as if a real person did them.

Some buttons are not wired up yet. If something does not respond, just say so and move on. That is
useful for us to know.

---

## If you are doing this in person

Someone will sit with you and give you one task at a time.

**Please think out loud as you go.** Say what you are looking at, what you expect to happen, and
what you are trying to do. It feels strange for the first minute and then it stops feeling strange.

If you go quiet, we will nudge you with "what are you thinking?" - we are not hurrying you, we just
cannot see inside your head.

We will not help unless you are completely stuck, and that is on purpose. Watching where people get
stuck is the whole point.

We would like to **record audio** so we do not have to scribble while you talk. No video, no names
in anything we write up, and the recording is deleted once the assignment is submitted. You can say
no and we will just take notes.

---

## If you are doing this on your own

1. Open the prototype link on your **phone** if you can. A laptop is fine if the phone is awkward.
2. Work through the five tasks below, in order, on your own.
3. Then fill in the form straight away, while it is still fresh.
4. Roughly note how long the tasks took you, and whether there was anything you could not finish.

The form asks you to rate some statements about how the app felt to use, a few questions specific to
ChronoMap, and two short written answers. It takes about five minutes.

---

## The tasks

Work through these in order. If you cannot finish one after a few minutes, move on - that is a
useful result, not a failure.

---

### Task 1

> A capture session is happening right now at the clock tower. Join it.

---

### Task 2

> You are in the session and the host has briefed everyone.
>
> Find out which position you have been given and where it is, then let the host know you are
> ready.

---

### Task 3

> Capture has started. The host asks you to move two metres to your left.
>
> Respond to them. Then tell us which side of the tower still needs photos.

---

### Task 4

> The capture is finished.
>
> Find the finished 3D model and share it to a story - but without revealing the exact location.

---

### Task 5

> During a session, you notice another participant is photographing strangers' faces.
>
> Do something about it.

---

## At the end

If you are doing this in person, we will ask you four quick questions:

- What was the most confusing moment?
- Would you join a session like this with people you do not know? Why, or why not?
- Would you use the voice channel, or the quick replies?
- Is there anything you would change?

Please be blunt. Polite feedback is lovely and completely useless to us. If something annoyed you,
tell us it annoyed you.

---

**Thank you.** Genuinely - ten people doing this is the difference between us guessing and us
knowing.
