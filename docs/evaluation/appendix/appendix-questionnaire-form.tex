\section{Questionnaire form (Google Forms)}
\label{app:form}

The querying method was a questionnaire built as a Google Form. Participants (PQ-1 to PQ-5) worked through
the five tasks alone, then answered the form straight afterwards. It is anonymous: the only identifier is the
participant code. The live form is at
\url{https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdk7cHsy_SmvPq-3zQoIdOEtMlXYxSNDaYZM05hK_fTmzMszA/viewform}.
Figures~\ref{fig:form1} to~\ref{fig:form5} show the five pages as participants saw them, signed out, with no
answers entered. The items follow the System Usability Scale (Part A), six items about ChronoMap (Part B), two
open questions (Part C) and questions about the participant and the session (Part D).
