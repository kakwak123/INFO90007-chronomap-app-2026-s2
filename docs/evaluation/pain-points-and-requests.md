# Participants' pain points and what they asked for

One page that joins three things: what hurt (the pain point), what participants said they want (in their own words, where they said it), and the change the team proposes. Sources: `think-aloud-notes.md`, `questionnaire-results.md` and `findings-log.md`. Codes only, no names.

**How to read it.** "Asked for" is only what a participant actually said. Where nobody asked for a fix, it says so, and the proposed change is the team's inference, not a participant request. Proposals are not final until the team agrees them, and none of the v2 changes below is built yet.

PT3 and PT4 are Dhiraj's labels for his two sessions (code recode pending). PT-8 is the 29 October session, on the earlier prototype.

## 2. What participants asked for, in short

Only requests a participant actually made:

1. **Explain the app and its purpose up front, and let people see the tutorial again** (PT-1, PT3).
2. **Make reporting easier to find and understand** (PT4).
3. **Offer anonymity and reassure people about safety and tracking** (PT3, PT4, PQ-4).
4. **Simplify the capture screens** (PT-8).
5. **A tap to respond, not speech, when around strangers** (PT-8: "a button that I can press"; PT-2, PT3 and PT4: quick replies with strangers, voice with people they know).

## 3. What participants liked

- The badge: PT4 had "a surprised and a happy reaction" to the Golden hour badge, and C6 (badge as motivation) is one of the highest questionnaire items.
- Overall mood: PQ-3 said "The app seems fun to use", PQ-7 "Nothing really confused me", and PQ-4 would be "a regular user" if safety is reassuring.
- Quick replies with strangers (see 5 above).
