\section*{AI Use Statement}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{AI Use Statement}

\noindent\textbf{Did you use artificial intelligence software such as ChatGPT, QuillBot or DALL\textperiodcentered E in
completing this assessment?}

\medskip
\noindent \DECIDE{Yes or No. Answer honestly for the group as a whole.}

\medskip
\noindent\FILL{If yes: 50--300 words explaining which tools were used, how they were used, how the
output was incorporated into the assignment, and why. Be specific about the division between
generated and authored material --- for instance, which screens were drafted with assistance and
which design decisions were made by the team. The design decision log records this, so the
statement can be accurate rather than vague.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Logs of interactions.}
\FILL{Shareable links to the conversation logs, one per tool or session. The handout asks for the
prompts and the outputs, not only a summary. Export these before submission --- links generated
after the deadline are no use.}
